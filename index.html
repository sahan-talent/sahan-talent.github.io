Why we built Sahan

Talent is everywhere in Somalia. Opportunity should be too.

Too many young Somalis finish school with real skills and no work to use them on. Freelancing apps exist in other countries, but they don't take Somali payments, so local workers are left out.

Sahan is your gateway to better work connections. Whether you're hiring or job hunting, Sahan makes it simple and effective, and pays out through the mobile money Somalia already uses.

2

Features

Everything you need to find work or get it done.

Vacancies and jobs

Clients post full vacancies or one-off jobs. Freelancers browse both in one place.

Search and filters

Search popular, nearby or recommended jobs, and filter by part-time, full-time and more.

Jobs matched to you

Sahan recommends jobs that match your profile, so the right work finds you.

Track your applications

Apply in a few taps and follow the status of every application you send.

Chat and file sharing

Message clients or freelancers one to one in real time and share files in the chat.

Reviews and top freelancers

Ratings and reviews build trust, and the best-rated freelancers rise to the top.

Verified and trusted

User verification helps keep fake accounts and scams off the platform.

Earnings and receipts

See your payment history, receipts and earnings from your profile.

Notifications

Get alerts for new jobs, application updates, messages and payments.

Local payments

No bank card needed. Just your phone.

Other freelancing platforms ask for international cards and foreign accounts. On Sahan you pick your mobile money, enter your +252 number, and confirm the payment on your phone.

Questions

What is Sahan?

Sahan Talent is a freelancing and job app for Somalia. People and businesses post vacancies and jobs, and freelancers apply, do the work and get paid through local mobile money.

Which payment methods are supported?

Sahan supports Somali mobile money, including EVC Plus and eDahab, so you do not need a bank card or an international account.

Can I find work with no experience?

Yes. Sahan has positions for people with no experience, so you can start building your profile and your reviews.
