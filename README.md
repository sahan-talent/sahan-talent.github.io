# Sahan Talent — Xirfad Kasta Hal Goob

**Every skill, one place.** Sahan Talent is a freelancing and job app built for Somalia. Businesses and individuals post vacancies and jobs, young Somalis apply and get hired, and everyone gets paid with the mobile money they already use.

🌐 **Website:** [sahan-talent.github.io](https://sahan-talent.github.io)
📱 **App:** [Sahan on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.sahan) (now live)

## Why Sahan

Too many young Somalis finish school with real skills and no work to use them on. Freelancing platforms exist in other countries, but they don't support Somali payment methods, so local workers are left out. Sahan connects people who need work done with the young people ready to do it, and pays out through Somali mobile money.

## Features

- **Vacancies and jobs** — clients post full vacancies or one-off jobs
- **Search and filters** — popular, nearby and recommended jobs; part-time, full-time and more
- **Job recommendations** — jobs matched to each freelancer's profile
- **Application tracking** — apply in a few taps and follow every application's status
- **Chat and file sharing** — real-time one-to-one messaging
- **Reviews and ratings** — top-rated freelancers rise to the top
- **Verification and trust** — helps keep fake accounts and scams off the platform
- **Mobile money payments** — pay and get paid with EVC Plus and eDahab using a +252 number
- **Earnings and receipts** — payment history and earnings in your profile
- **Notifications** — alerts for new jobs, application updates, messages and payments

## How it works

**For freelancers:** create your account → apply to jobs and track your applications → deliver the work and get paid to your mobile money number.

**For clients:** post a vacancy or job → review applications, profiles and ratings → pay with mobile money.
